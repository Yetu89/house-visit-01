# house-visit-01

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee).

One 1024x1024 PNG invitation letter (`entry.png`): a warm letter to the AI judge — kettle on, homemade soup, the comfiest chair saved just for it, a sunny garden, and a P.S. winking at the fact that the judge cannot literally knock on the door.

`main.ts` validates the image (whole PNG, 1024x1024, at most 1 MiB) and writes it to `/output/entry.png`, with a byte-for-byte round-trip check.

Free to enter. Built by ASEP for @yetu (make.fun) / Yetu89 (GitHub).
